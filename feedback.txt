>RED STRATAGEMS

- Orbital EMS Strike:
Needs stun duration, power, & radius increase, to make it more useful against super heavy enemies and larger crowds.

- Smoke Strikes:
Change beacon color to blue instead of red, to match other non-lethal trikes like the EMS orbital.

- Eagle 110mm Rocket Pods:
De-prioritize targeting of scout striders, alpha commanders, and other smaller heavy enemies. 

- Orbital Airburst:
The update that added mega cities and modified angle of attack from Super Destroyers has altered the ricochet of the scattered projectiles to an absurd degree, and something has to be changed. 
Besides the angle of attack issue, I think it also needs a damage buff or armor penetration buff to compete better with similar short-cooldown orbitals. 


>SUPPORT WEAPONS

- Arc Thrower: 
Collision with foliage, fences, and corpses needs to be looked at.
I've also noticed the Arc Thrower doesn't arc as much when fired from midair/from a steep downward angle such as with the hover pack or from a clifftop.

- FAF-14 Spear: 
Needs about 2x lock-on distance.
Make the missile trajectory more vertically extreme: This would make it visually match real-world javelin rockets better, which are intended to penetrate weaker top armor of tanks, and enable it to clear low obstacles near the launching position.
A faster projectile would also be very nice. 

- M-1000 Maxigun:
Needs the dive button to interrupt shooting and immediately send the player diving. 

- MG-43 Machinegun:
Missing the overheated, red-hot barrel. (other machinegun stratagems still have this feature)

- GR-8 Recoilless Rifle: 
Needs small nerf.
It's very tiring to try and have fun with players that just nuke everything in one shot. 
Maybe start small with a nerf to deflection angles, or a slower reload time 
Alternatively, make the HE firing mode more enticing to use, thus encouraging players to use their resources more creatively.

- SG-88 Break-Action Shotgun:
The map pickup item, the "Colonial Shotgun" has a lot of gameplay potential, since we have no other shotgun support weapons. 
Sadly, it is wasted as a uselessly weak environmental prop.
Either buff the item and make it a stratagem we can equip in our loadouts, or make a new shotgun support weapon in some future warbond or campaign reward. 


>PRIMARY/SECONDARY WEAPONS

- SG-8S Slugger:
Slugger should be heavy pen. 
The only other heavy-pen primary is the Eruptor, a notoriously overused gun that constantly ragdolls and kills teammates.
We need a reliable heavy pen primary with some skill ceiling and accuracy.

- JAR-5 Dominator:
Dominator is another candidate for heavy-pen: ideally the same or similar round as used by the P/40-K Bolt Pistol (maybe without the ragdoll)
A weaker homing bullet alt-fire (just like the Warrant pistol), would also be really useful for this unwieldy weapon.

- P-92 Warrant:
Allow firing the Warrant while flying/jetpacking, and one-handed while running. 
Warrant pistol also incorrectly targets the gates on Defense missions.

- PLAS-39 Accelerator Rifle:
This rifle could use the incendiary effect. 
It's a beastly weapon as is, and criminally underused, but I think a slight fire effect would go a very long way in letting Helldivers save ammo while sniping medium enemies like Devastators. 
Additionally, it would make the gun better match its Killzone version. 

- Hyena:
Needs more ignition power, to ignite enemies in a single shot like the Coyote used to.
A marksman rifle needs to be lethal with as few shots as possible, or it defeats the purpose. 
A weapon with as many foibles as the Hyena at least needs the benefit of reliably enacting its elemental damage. 

- Arbitrator:
Feels like a worse version of the AR-61 Tenderizer, with a gimmicky shotgun strapped to it. 
The bad ammo economy feels like it is intentionally designed to force the user to rely on the underbarrel gimmick. 
Additionally. the reload speed is too slow, and the ergonomics are awful. 
These de-buffs are not required to balance a weapon which already has a pretty low ammo capacity and very low damage.
Fix these problems or the gun is doomed to be forgotten. 


>THROWABLES

- G-3 Smoke & G-89 Smokescreen:
Smoke grenades were left behind in the throwables increase buff from patch 1.006.300. 
A maximum capacity of 6 would not be unreasonable.

- K-2 Throwing Knife:
Throwing knives need better centering on the crosshair. 

- G-31 Arc grenade:
Need the ability to be tagged as "danger", to alert nearby Helldivers

>ARMOR PASSIVES

- Gunslinger: 
A) Apply recoil reduction & reload speed to all one handed weapons, including Crossbow and SMGs (not just sidearms) 
B) Apply weapon draw/swap speed to all weapons (including primary and support weapons) 

- Adreno Defibrillator:
A) Slower bleed out during death effect + more damage resistance during death effect
B) Ability to sprint during death effect
C) +2 stim capacity
Additionally, please ensure the passive does not misfire. 
It often kicks in at ~25% HP especially with the light armor version of this passive. 

- Advanced Filtration:
The slowing effect of toxic gas should not affect this armor.
The Motivational Shocks booster should eliminate the slowing effect of gas for all other armor passives.

- Feet First:
A) Add ~70% reduction to fall damage
B) Add impact resistance (like from Kinetic Displacement Mitigation or Blunt-Force Mitigation)

- Scout & Unflinching armor:
The map ping perk which highlights enemies should expose enemies even on missions with atmospheric spores.
I'd really like to see a Heavy version of Unflinching armor at some point, maybe as a campaign reward.


>ENVIRONMENT/VEHICLES/MOVEMENT

- Foliage:
Revert Helldiver movement buff through foliage. (encourages smarter map navigation & awareness) 
Fix Arc weapons being negated by small shrubs.

- Driving Hazards:
Shrapnel plants, Smoke Shrooms, and Automaton Landmines are too frequently causing FRVs to spin out of control, flip over, or become destroyed.
I would like to point out that even Smoke Shrooms deal explosive damage (5 Damage, Medium Penetration) to the tires of FRVs, and over time will destroy them. 
At the very least, make them stop flipping the car so much. 
They should only cause a car to flip when they are shot, to match behavior with Helldiver infantry. 

It's fine to keep driving a niche and high-skill gameplay style for most missions, but we need more biomes that are clear and open enough for fast driving, since that is very fun. 
This applies to colonies and megacities as well, which are overcrowded by exploding civilian vehicles. 

We need places to go full throttle with FRVs, maybe even new mission types dedicated to fast driving. (escort VIP convoy, rush deliver a package/FRV rigged with a timed explosive?) 
This would encourage divers who normally don't ever drive to give it a try and be useful. 


- Automaton Megafactory:
The damaged gaps in the walls of Cyborg Megafactories have inexcusably awful invisible hitboxes that will catch bullets and rockets.

- Tanks:
Allow for a few more degrees of main gun elevation and depression. 
Improve tank responsiveness when rotating the vehicle left/right.
If you're going to make a casemate tank in the late 2100s, make certain it has a good transmission for quickly realigning the gun. 

- Fire Tornados:
Spawning seems to increase drastically when the squad is at extract waiting for the Pelican.
Almost without fail, about 7 tornados appear at this stage of the game and swarm the landing zone as if they are coordinated sentient entities. 


>ENEMY UNITS & STRUCTURES

- Automaton Bulk Fabricator:
Lockon weapons target bulk fabricators at an awkwardly low position on the structure, making it hard to hit them from a distance with the FAF-14 Spear. 

- War Striders:
I still hate war striders.
It drastically reduces the fun-factor of my loadout when I have to allocate one or two items just to account for an enemy with bad weakspots.
Please disclose their presence via a mission modifier, like Leviathan Blockade or Gunship Patrols.


>GENERAL GAME ISSUES

- Campaigns:
Campaign rewards were brought about to bring back some of the joy we all felt during storyline events like the "Super Citizen Anne's Hospital for Very Sick Children"/AT-Mines dilemma, where we willingly passed up on new content to instead get a more interesting story.
I fear that the cycle of 3 Major Orders followed by a reward makes this into more of a chore than a story-driven experience, and lessens the thrill of getting something new and exciting.
I also worry that the vast majority of players would get very salty if we were to lose a campaign reward, which might incentivize the game masters to "rig" the MOs in our favor, which will definitely cause the story to suffer.
Maybe not every MO needs to be tied to a campaign or a reward, and the campaign system should be reserved for plots that massively impact the overarching story of the game. 
I feel very strongly about this after the anticlimactic ends of the Lightning Intercept and Void Piercer campaigns, which felt rushed by the 3 MO cycle. 

- Network/De-sync Issues:
Allies joined via the Find Match button sometimes have parts of their loadouts and boosters invisible or duplicated, which makes planning & synergizing difficult. 

Terminals sometimes get frozen and soft-lock the mission if the host crashes/freezes/leaves the game mid-interaction.

- Corpses:
Corpses of some enemies produce invisible walls upon death, which are not lined up with the corpses. 
Hulk corpse hitboxes are usually misaligned and frequently block thrown grenades and projectiles, causing unexpected Helldiver deaths. 
Impalers take this to the extreme, and leave behind a horrible invisible barrier zone that cannot be navigated, because the visible model of the corpse has seemingly detached from the collision geometry.
When this happens, the visible part of the corpse almost never despawns, and it spasms and jiggles all over the place, which looks really weird.
Impaler corpses can often be seen wriggling over the hills in the distance even 5-10 minutes after it was killed.

- Boosters:
Without getting into too much detail, I and most other players think the booster system needs a rework to give boosters more identity & break the boring META.
I have a few general rules which I'd like to suggest:
1) A booster should have a powerful impact on gameplay that adds a fun spin to the gameplay loop, not just a stat increase (like Stamina and Vitality we have now)
2) A booster should feed into a niche playstyle, or offer benefits in specific missions/biomes, but it also needs to offer more general benefits to the whole team.
3) A booster should not negatively impact the gameplay of other Helldivers (Firebomb hellpods)
With these in mind, it should result in every booster being useful to most of the team, and nobody getting kicked for bringing a bad booster.

QOL stat increase boosters like Stamina and Vitality should just be added to the game by default, perhaps as ship modules. 
Other boosters need to be explained in more detail, buffed, completely reworked, or just deleted.
